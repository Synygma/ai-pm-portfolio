# Anatomy of a post (rules distilled from the research report)

Evidence tags: [LI] = LinkedIn's own statement, [DATA] = large third-party dataset, [EXP] = expert opinion.

## The six parts

| Part | Job | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook (lines 1–2) | Earn the "see more" tap from the right reader | Core idea within ≤140 chars (mobile fold; ~210 desktop; cut is partly line-based, ~3 lines, and a blank line counts as a line) [EXP]. Front-load a number, named tool, result or tension. Signal who it's for (AI PMs / AI builders). The body must pay it off without exaggeration. |
| 2. Context (1–3 lines) | Answer "why should I believe you?" | Scope and stakes: time spent, sample size, role, what was at risk. LinkedIn rewards insight from people with relevant expertise [LI 2023, 2026]. |
| 3. Body (3–5 beats) | Deliver the insight, hold dwell time | Paragraphs 1–3 lines (max 4). Vary rhythm: avoid a wall of one-liners ("broetry"). Lists only for real sets/sequences. One number per beat. Include at least one failure, caveat or trade-off. Open a loop early, close it late. Dwell time is a modelled signal [LI 2020]. |
| 4. Takeaway | Something repeatable and saveable | One sentence, quotable, slightly reframing or contrarian. Place in the last 2–3 lines. Test: would someone screenshot this line? |
| 5. CTA | Start a conversation the feed can rank | Ask for the reader's experience, not agreement. Or end on a clean statement for strong findings (Mollick posts: no question, 230–320 comments). Comments are ranked per viewer (Aug 2026), so generic replies get buried. |
| 6. First comment | Hold links, add depth | Link/resource + one extra detail. Reply to early comments with substance; threaded replies correlate with up to 2.4× reach [DATA AuthoredUp 2025]. |

## Length targets [DATA AuthoredUp, Mar 2025–Feb 2026]
- Text posts: 1,000+ chars → 1.18× reach; 700–800 → 0.98×; <300 → 0.88×; <5 sentences → 0.64×; 20+ sentences → 1.14×. Target 900–1,500 chars; hard cap 3,000.
- Exception: an author with authority and one sharp finding can win at 50–100 words (Mollick). Only go short when the finding is sharp and verified.
- Image captions: 800–900 chars (1.04×); >1,000 adds nothing.
- Document/carousel captions: 0–100 chars → 1.28×.
- Video: 3 min+ → 1.21× reach vs 30–60 s → 0.95×.

## Storytelling frameworks
- **Claim → Test → Result → Caveat**: prompting, evals, tool comparisons (most credible).
- **Expectation → Reality → Fix**: rollout post-mortems, enterprise adoption.
- **Setup → Numbered observations → Reflection**: workshops, build sprints, teaching.
- **Scene → Turn → Lesson**: adoption and human-impact stories.
- **Upside / Downside / Verdict**: new model or tool releases.

## Tone
- Polish the structure, not the voice. Keep the specific, slightly awkward details that prove you were there.
- Use a personal observation as evidence for a general insight. A story with no insight reads as a diary; an insight with no personal evidence reads as commentary.
- Credit named colleagues, papers and builders (posts that credit others outperform self-promotion [DATA Usera/UTA 2025–26]).
- Use domain vocabulary: golden set, handoff rate, eval drift, activation vs retention, cost per task.

## Banned phrases and patterns (cringe / AI cadence / bait)
- "🚨 BREAKING", "This changes everything", "game-changer", "revolutionize", "unlock", "in today's fast-paced world"
- "Humbled and honored to announce", "I'm thrilled to share"
- "Here's the thing:", "Let that sink in.", "Read that again.", "And that's okay.", "Most people don't know this, but…"
- "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Comment YES", "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll DM you", "Repost to help your network", "Follow me for more"
- One-word line + blank line + one-word line stacks that spread across the fold
- Reflexive triplets in every sentence, "not X, but Y" in every paragraph
- Hype claims with no test ("AI will replace PMs")

## Reach killers to avoid
- Engagement bait, pods, automated comments [LI Mar 2026]
- Video that doesn't match its caption [LI Mar 2026]
- Recycled takes [LI Mar 2026]
- Very short text from a small account (<5 sentences) [DATA]
- 6+ hashtags [DATA]; use 0–2
- A link as the post's main point (roughly −19% to −27% reach in most studies; contested) [DATA]
- Long captions on document posts [DATA]
- Off-lane topics that blur the profile–topic match [EXP]
