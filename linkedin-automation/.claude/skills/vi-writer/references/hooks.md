# Hook patterns for AI content

Each hook's core idea must land in ≤140 characters. Use the strong shape; avoid the weak one.

| Pattern | Weak | Strong shape | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test result | "Prompt engineering tips you need to know 👇" | "We tested [common belief] on [real sample]. [Result that contradicts it]." | Specific, falsifiable, challenges a belief (Mollick persona-prompting post: 1,834 reactions / 255 comments). |
| Number + consequence | "AI is transforming enterprises." | "Our [rollout] hit [metric A]. [Metric B] was [surprising number] by [time]." | The gap between two numbers is the story. |
| Paradox | "AI experts are important." | "The best [role] on my team was the worst at [related thing]." | Short; the reader has to continue to resolve it (Mollick, ~50 words: 1,683 / 232). |
| Build log | "I built something cool with AI!" | "I built [thing] with [tool] in [time]. It [good outcome] and [failure]." | Cost + outcome + failure signals honesty. |
| Scene | "AI is for everyone." | "[Specific person, age/role] [did specific AI thing] [when]. [Unexpected detail]." | A concrete human detail (Allie K. Miller's 64-year-old story). |
| Prediction with stake | "2026 will be the year of AI agents." | "[Bounded, checkable prediction] within [timeframe]. Here's what I'm seeing in [first-hand places]." | Prediction backed by observation (Allie's voice-AI post: 2,801 / 462). |
| Fresh-news verdict | "BREAKING: OpenAI just released a new model 🚨" | "I had [access/usage] to [model] for [time]. [Better at X], [worse at Y]. Details:" | Access + two-sided verdict (30 comments per 100 reactions, best ratio in the sample). |

## 5-second hook test
1. Contains a number, named tool or named situation?
2. Could only someone who did the work have written it?
3. Tension opens above the fold and resolves below it?
4. An AI PM would recognize it as being for them?
5. True with no exaggeration?

## Fold mechanics
- About 140 chars mobile / 210 desktop is a third-party estimate; the cut is partly line-based (about 3 lines).
- A blank line after line 1 uses up a full line of preview. Keep lines 1–2 tight.
- Don't put the conclusion in the hook; put the tension there.
