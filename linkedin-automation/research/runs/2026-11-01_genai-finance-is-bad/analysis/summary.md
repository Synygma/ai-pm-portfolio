# Summary
- **What's happening:** 2025-26 finance benchmarks (Vals Finance Agent v2, Fin-RATE) show LLMs doing well at retrieval and summaries but failing at modeling and multi-step analysis. Gartner's 2026 survey shows value lagging expectations in finance teams.
- **Why now:** Vals v2 category results (Aug 2026) make the gap measurable by task type.
- **Who's affected:** CFOs, FP&A and accounting teams, and PMs building finance AI products.
- **PM decision:** ship retrieval and drafting use cases now; keep calculations and forecast inputs on deterministic tools with human sign-off.
- **Angle chosen:** "GenAI isn't bad at finance. It's bad at the part where one wrong number costs money."
