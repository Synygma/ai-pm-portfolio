# Finance Agent Benchmark: Benchmarking LLMs on Real-world Financial Research Tasks

- Publisher: arXiv (Vals AI team) · Authors: Bigeard, Nashold, Krishnan, Wu
- Published: 2025-05-20 (verified on page)

## Summary
Benchmark of 537 expert-authored questions modelled on real financial-analyst research tasks. Agents were given tools such as search and filings access.

## Key facts (exact)
- "The dataset includes 537 expert-authored questions"
- "even the best-performing model (OpenAI o3) achieved only 46.8% accuracy at an average cost of $3.79 per query"

## Caveats / methodology notes
A search snippet quoted 48.3% / $3.69 (likely an earlier version); the abstract figures were used. v1 scores are not comparable with v2.
