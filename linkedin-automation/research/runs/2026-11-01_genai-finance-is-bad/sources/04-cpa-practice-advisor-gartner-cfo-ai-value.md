## Summary
Gartner's March 2026 survey of 204 finance leaders, presented at Finance Symposium/Xpo 2026. Adoption is moving into production, but realized value lags expectations.

## Key facts (exact)
- 204 finance leaders, surveyed March 2026
- 66% of AI-adopting finance orgs cite efficiency/productivity as a top benefit
- 63% said AI implementation was slower than expected in 2025
- Financial forecasting and insight generation are among the lowest-rated use cases for impact
- Marco Steecker: "They must not mistake activity for impact."
