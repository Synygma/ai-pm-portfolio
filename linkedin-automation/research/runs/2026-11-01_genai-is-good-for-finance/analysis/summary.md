# Summary
- **What happened:** UBS's Q3 2026 results (28 Oct) disclosed 12,700+ employee-run AI personal agents and 608 live AI use cases (+81% YoY). Two days earlier (26 Oct), the Bank of England/FCA 2026 survey reported 86% of UK financial firms using AI, GenAI at 56% of AI systems, and 80% with agents deployed but only 2% with broad autonomy.
- **Why it matters now:** this is the clearest evidence yet that GenAI has scaled inside banks, and it shows where it has scaled: assistive tools with a human deciding.
- **Who's affected:** bank technology and product leaders, PMs building internal AI platforms, finance-function leaders.
- **PM decision:** build the platform for employee-built assistive agents (an owner per agent, scoped data access, a human-executes default, an outcome metric per agent defined before launch) so value can be reported, not just counts.
- **Angle chosen:** "GenAI is good for finance, as an assistant at scale, not as an autonomous analyst. It has won the adoption argument; the next one is proof."
- **Format:** image post. Stat card: 12,700 agents · 608 use cases (+81%) · 80% have agents · 2% broad autonomy.
