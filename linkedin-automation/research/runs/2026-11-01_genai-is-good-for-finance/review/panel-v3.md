## Required corrections
- C3: replace "That's roughly one for every nine people at the bank." with "That's roughly one for every eight employees (97,593 full-time staff)." The alternative is "roughly one for every nine people, counting contractors." Reason: dividing internal FTE by 12,700 gives 7.7, not 9. Source: UBS 3Q26 release, personnel table.
- C9: replace "80% of UK firms have AI agents deployed." with "80% of firms surveyed have AI agents deployed." Reason: the base is survey respondents, not all UK firms. Source: BoE/FCA 2026.
- C7 (optional but cleaner): replace "86% of UK financial firms use AI" with "86% of the UK financial firms surveyed use AI". Same reason.
- C11: replace "So yes, GenAI is good for finance." with "So yes, I think GenAI is good for finance." Reason: this is an evaluative claim and needs first-person framing (my-views rule 2).
- C12: replace "The value comes from thousands of small assistants that help people search, prepare and draft while a human decides." with "In my view, the value comes from thousands of small assistants that support people while a human decides." Reason: neither source measures value or names these tasks.
- C14: replace "The gap is proof." with "To me, that gap is the tell." Reason: the survey gap doesn't prove where value comes from.
- C19 (if alt hook 2 is used): replace "Not one dollar of savings attributed to them." with "UBS's results don't put a dollar figure on them." Reason: verified only for the Q3 release.
