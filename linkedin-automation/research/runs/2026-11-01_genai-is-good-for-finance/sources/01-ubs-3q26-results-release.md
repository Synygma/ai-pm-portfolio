## Summary (3–5 lines)
UBS's Q3 2026 results release. Net profit was USD 2.7bn. The release has a dedicated AI paragraph: 9 large-scale "end-to-end transformational AI initiatives", 608 live AI use cases (+81% YoY), 1,090+ more in development, and over 12,700 "AI personal agents" run by employees. Over 18,000 developers use AI tools, and the STAAT advisor tool produced 30M+ insights YTD. The release also reports integration cost saves (cumulative USD 13.2bn) but does not attribute any of them to AI.

## Key facts (exact numbers and wording)
- "We continue to progress our 9 large-scale, end-to-end transformational AI initiatives and build on our 608 live AI use cases in production (81% YoY increase, with another 1,090+ applications in development)."
- "Our employees are running over 12,700 AI personal agents to support them in their daily activities."
- "Over 18,000 of our software developers are leveraging AI tools in the software development cycles, leading to measurable productivity gains." (no number given for the gains)
- "our award-winning STAAT tool has year-to-date generated over 30 million insights to be distributed to the Financial Advisor teams"
- "we're the leading bank in Europe in the 2026 Evident AI Index for financial services and the only European bank in the global top 10"; "first place globally for Talent Development"
- Net profit USD 2.7bn; cost/income ratio 72.3% reported, 68.7% underlying
- Gross cost savings: USD 0.6bn in 3Q26, cumulative USD 13.2bn; now expecting ~USD 13.7bn by end-2026 (previous ambition ~USD 13.5bn)
- Personnel (internal + external) at 30 Sep 2026: 109,990
- "We remain focused on investing in technology, including AI, as well as our capabilities and talent..."

## Caveats / methodology notes
- The release attributes no dollar savings to AI. The cost saves come from the Credit Suisse integration program.
- "Measurable productivity gains" for developers is not quantified.
- The release does not split agents or use cases into customer-facing and internal, and says nothing about how the agents are governed.
- Published 2026-10-28, about 4 days before the run date, so it is outside the strict 72h window. In-window coverage: PYMNTS (10-29), AI Weekly (10-30), AI Agents Directory brief (10-31).
