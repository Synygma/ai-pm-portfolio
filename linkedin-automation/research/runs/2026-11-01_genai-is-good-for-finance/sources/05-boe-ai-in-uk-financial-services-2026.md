## Summary (3–5 lines)
The fourth joint BoE/FCA survey of AI use in UK financial services. Adoption has risen to 86%, and GenAI now makes up the majority of reported AI systems. Agents are widespread, but almost all of them are decision support with a human executing; only 2% report broad autonomy. Third-party dependence is rising.

## Key facts (exact numbers and wording)
- 86% of respondents use AI (75% in 2024)
- 196 respondents (101 larger, 95 smaller firms); 105 completed the full survey, and most figures use this subset
- GenAI = 56% of reported AI systems (foundation models were 17% in 2024)
- 80% have agents deployed (decision support: humans decide and execute); a further 14% plan to within 12 months; 2% report agents with broad autonomy
- Third-party implementations: 39% of AI systems (33% in 2024); 53% of high-materiality systems (35% in 2024)
- Highest-rated benefits: operational efficiency, automation, productivity (no % given in the part read)
- Highest-rated risks: data protection/security, third-party/supply chain, GenAI-specific (hallucination, prompt injection)
