## Summary (3–5 lines)
A law-firm digest of the BoE/FCA 2026 AI survey. It shows the survey was still being analyzed inside the window and adds concentration and governance figures.

## Key facts (exact numbers and wording)
- 86% use AI (75% in 2024, 58% in 2022); n = 196
- GenAI 56% of reported AI systems; operations and IT ~31% of deployments
- Top 10 respondents = 43% of all AI systems and 62% of high-materiality systems
- 80% deploying agents, 14% planning within a year; 2% broad autonomy; <10% expect agents in high-impact activities even in planned deployments
- Three largest cloud/compute providers = 79% of provider relationships; three largest model API providers = 54%
- 94% have firm-wide AI policies; 84% centralised governance forums; 77% claim deep technical understanding of internal systems vs 24% for third-party systems
- 80% expect frontier AI cyber capabilities to have moderate/high impact on their risk profile within 2 years

## Caveats / methodology notes
- Secondary to the BoE report (source 05). The figures are consistent with it.
