## Summary (3–5 lines)
Argues that banks are moving from usage and hours-saved metrics to return metrics, with BofA, SocGen and Commerzbank figures and PYMNTS survey data. Published outside the window, so it is used only as context and baseline.

## Key facts (exact numbers and wording)
- BofA (Moynihan, Sept 14 conference): 130–140 AI projects cost $400M and generated $800M in benefit (no period stated); plans to double AI expense budget next year
- BofA: EricaAssist used by 18,000+ customer service reps cuts average call times by nearly a minute; without Erica the bank would need 11,000 more people (CTIO Hari Gopalkrishnan)
- SocGen: up to €600M of €1.9bn 2026–2029 gross savings from AI (target)
- Commerzbank: ~€350M/yr AI savings by 2030; ~€500M yearly AI value from 2030 (target)
- PYMNTS Intelligence "Enterprise AI Payback Curve" (n = 60 senior tech execs, US firms ≥$1bn revenue): 90–100% report some return; only 5–10% report full payback; at least half in every industry group expect full payback to take 5–6 years

## Caveats / methodology notes
- BofA figures have no stated period. SocGen and Commerzbank figures are targets, not realized results. n = 60 is small.
