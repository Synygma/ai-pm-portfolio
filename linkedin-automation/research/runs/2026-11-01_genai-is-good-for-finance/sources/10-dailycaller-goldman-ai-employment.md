## Summary (3–5 lines)
Goldman economists found that industries with higher AI adoption showed weaker employment growth. The effects extend into finance and insurance.

## Key facts (exact numbers and wording)
- A 10-pp increase in industry-level AI adoption was associated with ~0.9 pp weaker employment growth and a 0.9 pp higher unemployment measure (vs 2015–2019 industry average)
- Statistically significant; data from Census Bureau, BLS, IPUMS; correlation, not causation
- Sectors include financial services, insurance, telecom, real estate, professional services
- Census (May): ~34% of finance and insurance firms use AI vs ~20% of all US businesses
