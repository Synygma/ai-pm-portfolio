## Summary (3–5 lines)
A roundup of US big-bank CEO comments on AI from Q3 2026 calls. Outside the window; used as peer baseline.

## Key facts (exact numbers and wording)
- Citi (Fraser): average 30–40% increase in product launch speed; 14,000 engineers use agentic coding tool Devin; reengineering 100 manual processes with AI; "We've reduced our tech vendor spend by 30% year-over-year as a consequence..."
- JPMorgan (Dimon): wants to avoid "rogue agents — and there could be thousands of them"; "You don't need the most expensive thing for every query you have."
- Goldman (Solomon): "the choice is not between halting progress and moving forward without guardrails."
- Morgan Stanley (Pick): "do I trust my financial adviser?"
