ParCare test version

Stay part of your parent's day when you live apart. With the parent's agreement, an AI companion checks in at times they choose: waking up, medicine, a walk. The child gets an evening summary in the parent's own words, and an alert if check-ins go unanswered.

Open ParCare
Not an emergency or medical service. Please use made-up names and details only.

If you are the child

You start here and invite your "parent".

  1. Open parcare-six.vercel.app in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
  2. Tap I am the child → Create account → tick that you agree.
  3. Follow the setup: your details, your parent's details, describe their day (talk or type).
  4. At Invite link, send the link to the person playing your parent.
  5. Turn on notifications in Settings.

If you are the parent

You got a link from your "child".

Android

  1. Touch and hold the link → Copy. Paste it into Chrome.
  2. Tap Open my ParCare.
  3. Chrome ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen. Use the new icon from now on.

iPhone

  1. In Safari, open parcare-six.vercel.app → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Touch and hold the link in the message → Copy.
  3. Open the new ParCare icon → I am the parent → paste the link → Open → Open my ParCare.

Then

  • Choose your companion, describe your day, tick the check-ins you agree to.
  • Say Allow to notifications and the microphone.
  • Answer check-ins in the chat: tap the buttons, type or talk.
  • Use Ask for help to send a request to your child.

Each parent link works once. If it says "already used", ask your child for a new one: Settings → link for the parent's phone.

Already using ParCare? Close it and open it again to get the latest version.
